Am I in danger? A quick check

You may be at higher risk if your partner or ex-partner: has threatened to kill you or your children; has strangled, choked or used a weapon against you; monitors your phone, messages or movements; has recently separated from you or you are planning to leave; or you feel afraid to go home. If any of this sounds familiar, please call the National Domestic Abuse Helpline on 0808 2000 247 — it's free and they will listen without judgement.

How do I browse safely / clear my history?

On most phones and computers you can open a private/incognito window, or clear your browser history afterwards via Settings. If someone might check your device, consider using a library, a friend's phone, or the Exit Site button at the top of this page, which takes you straight to a neutral page.

What is domestic abuse?

Domestic abuse isn't only physical. It can include emotional, financial, sexual or coercive control — a pattern of behaviour used to frighten, isolate or control a partner, ex-partner or family member. If something doesn't feel right, it's worth talking it through, even if you're not sure it "counts."
