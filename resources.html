Resources & factsheets

Materials for survivors, children and young people, and professionals — part of the #NoExcuseForAbuse campaign run with police forces, local councils and support agencies across the region.

Public factsheets

Available in English, Arabic, Bulgarian, Latvian, Polish, Punjabi, Romanian and Urdu, so language is never a barrier to understanding what domestic abuse is and where to get help.

Professional factsheets

Guidance tailored for employees and employers, frontline professionals, and other service providers who may come into contact with someone experiencing abuse.

Resources for children & young people

Age-appropriate materials to help children and young people understand abuse and know how to get help, used alongside our young people's services.

Resources for adults

Guidance and self-help materials for adults affected by domestic abuse, whether in crisis now or further along in recovery.
