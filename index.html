The Wish Centre has supported anyone affected by domestic abuse in Blackburn with Darwen since 1989 — through refuge, outreach, counselling and education. Whoever you are, there's a clear way in below.

35+ years supporting survivors

Founded in 1989 as Blackburn and Darwen District Women's Aid, we opened our first refuge in 1991 and have grown into a full service offering refuge, IDVA support, counselling, recovery programmes and education — for women, children and families.
