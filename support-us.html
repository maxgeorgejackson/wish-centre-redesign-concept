Your support funds safety, recovery and independence

From emergency alarms to therapeutic support for children in refuge, every donation has a direct, visible impact.

£100an essentials pack for a family coming into refuge

What partners receive

  • Our expertise in domestic abuse, honour-based violence, forced marriage and FGM, plus policy and staff champion training
  • Positive reputation and social responsibility recognition with your staff and community

  • Staff volunteering opportunities that build team cohesion
