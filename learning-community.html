Learning & Community

Training that helps people spot the signs, and respond well

We work with schools, colleges, employers and professionals across Blackburn with Darwen to build understanding of domestic abuse and confidence to respond to it safely.

Enquire about training

HR & employer training

1 in 4 adults in the UK will experience domestic abuse at some point in their life. We equip HR teams and managers to recognise warning signs, understand the impact on employees, and respond with compassion and practical guidance — building safer, more supportive workplaces.

Schools & colleges

Healthy relationships education and workshops for young people, plus safeguarding training for teachers and youth workers, delivered in partnership with Blackburn with Darwen Borough Council.

Bespoke & CPD training

Ready-made domestic abuse training packages, or bespoke training developed for your organisation on request — suitable for CPD.

Community programmes

Engagement activities and recovery programmes delivered across the community, supporting long-term independence beyond the point of crisis.
