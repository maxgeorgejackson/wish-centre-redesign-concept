# Homepage surface brief

Scope: homepage (`src/pages/index.astro`); the world it sets carries to every page. Mode: Persuade, but the persuading is "call us / you can get help", never "donate".

Audience and job: someone affected by domestic abuse, possibly on a monitored phone, finds a number to call or leaves instantly. Secondary: professionals booking training, supporters giving. Proof on hand: since 1989, refuge/IDVA/counselling, accreditations, real photos in `public/images/`. Constraints: help before fundraising; not clinical or corporate; no third-party scripts; exit button and Esc on every page.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Help is the first bolt of cloth on the shelf. The page refuses the category default of a purple hero photo with a helpline banner; the number itself is the hero.

OWN-WORLD: Fabric-shop wall of flat dyed cotton: deep teal ground, madder pink, saffron orange and indigo bolts, unbleached cotton for reading areas. A printed selvedge strip (small caps, repeating) runs at the top of each cloth field and carries wayfinding. Marigold is reserved for calling and exiting only. Flat sealed fields, no gradients or shadows; square edges; sections meet like folded cloth edges.

STORY: Visitor sees "talk to us" and the number within a second, trusts it is local and confidential, taps to call or picks their door; supporters and trainers find their own bolt without crossing help.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Black exit bar full width (marigold Exit button right). Logo + nav on teal. Selvedge strip. Left 60%: display headline, one-line reassurance, marigold call button with 0300 561 0440 at near-display size, hours and the 24h helpline + 999 beneath. Right 40%: three tall vertical bolts (pink: I need help; saffron: Training; indigo: Support us) running to the bottom edge.

FORM: Cloth Shop, position 5 of 7 on my ordered list (Blackburn fabric shops); seed:c494402d9728b45a. Signature interaction: bolts unroll (height/clip reveal) on hover/focus to show their sub-links; reduced motion shows them at rest.
