Our services, in full

A complete picture for professionals, partners and anyone wanting the detail behind "I Need Help" and "Learning & Community." If you're in crisis right now, the short version is on our I Need Help page.

Refuge

Safe, self-contained flats or houses with a shared garden and 24-hour CCTV, for women and children fleeing abuse. Self-refer or ask an agency to refer you: 01254 55300.

Outreach support

Community-based support to rebuild confidence and sustain a tenancy — covering welfare benefits, housing, employment, and the justice system.

IDVA service

Accredited Independent Domestic Violence Advocates offering safety and wellbeing planning, trauma-informed court accompaniment, help preparing victim impact statements, and home safety assessments. Includes a specialist BAME IDVA, an Independent Stalking Advocacy Caseworker, an Over 55s service ("Open Doors"), and Operation Provide working alongside police. Referrals via the Adult Initial Referral Form.

Young people's services

Young People's Programmes, a Young Person's IDVA, education work in schools, and a dedicated "Get Help" route for young people experiencing abuse themselves.

Counselling

Eight 50-minute confidential sessions, in Blackburn or online via Teams, for anyone aged 18+, living in Blackburn with Darwen, who has experienced domestic abuse and is no longer in immediate crisis. Delivered by qualified and trainee volunteer counsellors. Contact: andrea.ryan@thewishcentre.org.

Recovery programmes

AIM (Awareness, Insight, Motivation) helps survivors understand the beliefs held by abusive partners, recognise similar beliefs they may have internalised, and build safety strategies for themselves and their children — run as a group for women, and individually for male survivors.

Recovery Toolkit runs up to 22 sessions building practical coping skills and resilience. Both programmes are open to Blackburn with Darwen residents.

Behaviour change programmes

Make The Change is a voluntary, 16–22 week behaviour change programme (not anger management) for men who recognise they are using power and control to abuse a female partner. It's challenging and participative, grounded in Duluth and integrated domestic abuse models, and supported by Blackburn with Darwen Borough Council. Self-referral or professional referral to info@thewishcentre.org.

Training

Ready-made or bespoke domestic abuse training for organisations, including HR & employer training and CPD. See Learning & Community for more.
