Volunteers & peer mentors

Volunteering is a great way to gain experience and support your local community. Our volunteers and peer mentors bring a diverse range of skills to help us deliver vital services for survivors and their families — with comprehensive training, support and guidance in return. If you have a few hours spare each week, or would like to help us fundraise occasionally, we'd love to hear from you.

Student counsellors

We welcome counselling student placements. All student counsellors attend a full-day induction (held quarterly, with around 2–4 students per intake), complete online training modules, and provide DBS, insurance, fitness-to-practice and supervisor details. Most work with up to 4 clients a week, face-to-face in Blackburn or online. We welcome difference and diversity in modality, age, gender, language and ethnicity.

We are not currently recruiting student counsellors, but the Counselling Coordinator, Andrea Ryan, will contact applicants when places become available: andrea.ryan@thewishcentre.org.

Become an ally

Can't commit to regular volunteer hours? Our trustees invite people to become Allies instead — supporting us when you can, without a regular time commitment, by:

  • Helping raise awareness and signposting people to our service
  • Helping establish partnerships and raise funds
  • Being a public face of our organisation and generating media coverage

Allies get support and guidance plus regular updates from a trustee. Get in touch to find out more.

Safeguarding and safer recruitment

All roles — paid and voluntary — are subject to safer recruitment checks, including references and a DBS check appropriate to the role. We are committed to equal opportunities and welcome applications from all sections of the community.
